# Changelog

## 0.1.0

### Added

- Public booking site: service, employee, date and time selection, and contact info, with returning-customer auto-fill by phone number.
- Admin authentication and business management.
- Admin calendar with day and week views, manual appointment creation from the toolbar or directly from a calendar slot, and walk-in bookings outside configured hours with a warning.
- Recurring appointments, including a conflict preview before creation and automatic cancellation or recreation of future occurrences when a rule is deactivated or reactivated.
- Customer management, including ban and unban with automatic removal from the calendar.
- Appointment history view with filtering.
- Employee permissions management.
- WhatsApp notifications for booking confirmation, cancellation, and reminders.
- Revenue tracking with day, week, and month totals.
- Price visibility toggle for the public booking site.
- Audit log viewer for reviewing account activity.
- Spanish language support.
- Costa Rica phone number format check for booking submissions.
